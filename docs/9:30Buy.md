# Functional Requirements Document: 9:30 OPT-Buy Strategy

This document is the behavioral contract for live trading, paper trading, and

or implementation decision and recorded before release.

## 2. Executive Summary

The strategy scans the Nifty 50 option chain at 09:25 IST and selects one CE
and one PE whose premiums are closest to INR 180. From 09:30 IST, the selected
options are monitored using live ticks. The first side that crosses and remains
above INR 180 for the configured confirmation period is bought at market. The
other side is cancelled or disabled so that no more than one trade is entered
per day.

The position has a fixed 20-point stop loss and a fixed 40-point target,
calculated from the actual average fill price. Positions that are flat or
losing at 09:45 IST are exited immediately. A profitable position may remain
open until the unconditional 10:00 IST exit, unless its stop loss or target is
hit first.

## 3. Scope and Assumptions

1. All strategy timestamps are interpreted in IST, including broker timestamps
   normalized to IST before comparison.
2. The scan executes once per trading day. The scan is skipped on exchange
   holidays, weekends, or when required market data is unavailable.
3. “Current weekly expiry” means the nearest tradable weekly expiry on or after
   the trading date. If the nearest expiry is not tradable, the broker’s next
   tradable weekly expiry is used and the selection is logged.
4. A trade means a broker-confirmed filled BUY entry, including a partial fill.
   A rejected, cancelled, or never-filled entry does not consume the daily
   trade allowance unless broker reconciliation shows that a fill occurred.
5. The strategy uses the actual average fill price for SL and target calculation.
6. Prices are rounded to the exchange tick size before order submission.
7. The strategy does not hold positions or pending entries beyond 10:00 IST.

## 5. Functional Requirements

### 5.1 Session Initialization

| ID | Requirement | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| FR-01 | The system shall create one isolated strategy session per exchange trading day and reset the daily trade lock at session initialization. | Must |
| FR-02 | The system shall verify that the clock, broker connection, credentials, and required market-data channels are available before scanning. | Must |
| FR-03 | The system shall use the exchange trading calendar to skip non-trading days and shall not infer a trading day from a local process restart. | Must |
| FR-04 | On restart, the system shall reconcile broker positions and orders before creating new orders. An unknown open position shall block new entries until resolved. | Must |

### 5.2 Data Ingestion and 09:25 Selection

| ID | Requirement | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| FR-05 | At 09:25:00 IST, the system shall fetch a timestamped Nifty 50 spot quote and record its source and age. | Must |
| FR-06 | The system shall calculate ATM as the nearest valid strike using the configured strike step. Exact half-step ties shall round to the higher strike. | Must |
| FR-07 | The system shall resolve the nearest tradable current weekly expiry before constructing option symbols. | Must |
| FR-08 | The system shall obtain CE and PE quotes from the option chain for the selected expiry. Each side shall be selected independently based on minimum absolute distance from INR 180. | Must |
| FR-09 | The selection algorithm shall search only strikes within the configured selection range around ATM and shall exclude instruments with stale, zero, invalid, or non-tradable quotes. | Must |
| FR-10 | The system shall record the spot price, ATM strike, expiry, candidate symbols, candidate premiums, selected symbols, and selection distance. | Must |
| FR-11 | If either side has no valid candidate within the configured maximum selection distance, the system shall abort the session without placing an entry order. | Must |
| FR-12 | After a successful scan, exactly one CE and one PE shall be placed on the active watchlist. The watchlist shall not change after 09:30 unless the session is aborted. | Must |

The default maximum selection distance is 10 premium points. This prevents a
thin or abnormal option chain from silently selecting an option far from the
intended INR 180 reference.

### 5.3 Trigger and Signal Generation

| ID | Requirement | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| FR-13 | The system shall begin evaluating watchlist ticks at or after 09:30:00 IST and shall ignore ticks before that time. | Must |
| FR-14 | A side shall enter confirmation when its valid LTP first becomes strictly greater than INR 180. | Must |
| FR-15 | The side shall be considered confirmed only if valid LTP remains strictly greater than INR 180 continuously for `sustain_duration`. A tick at or below INR 180 shall reset confirmation. | Must |
| FR-16 | Ticks older than `quote_stale_after`, duplicated, malformed, or out of order shall not advance confirmation. | Must |
| FR-17 | The system shall not accept a new entry at or after 09:45:00 IST. A trigger confirmation that completes after the cutoff shall be ignored. | Must |
| FR-18 | If both sides become eligible, the side whose confirmation completion timestamp is earlier shall win. If timestamps are equal, CE shall win as the deterministic tie-breaker. | Must |
| FR-19 | Once an entry attempt starts, the global daily trade lock shall be acquired atomically before sending the order. | Must |
| FR-20 | The system shall record the crossing timestamp, confirmation completion timestamp, last confirmation quote, and reason for every accepted or rejected trigger. | Must |

### 5.4 Entry, OCO, and Risk Management

| ID | Requirement | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| FR-21 | The system shall submit one market BUY order for the winning option using the configured `entry_quantity` (default 130 units). The quantity must satisfy broker lot-size rules; an invalid quantity shall abort the entry. | Must |
| FR-22 | The system shall use the broker-confirmed average fill price, not the trigger quote, for all risk calculations. | Must |
| FR-23 | Immediately after an entry fill, the system shall calculate `SL = fill_price - 20` and `TP = fill_price + 40`, rounded to valid tick size. | Must |
| FR-24 | The system shall place protective stop and target orders immediately after fill and shall associate both orders with the position and strategy session. | Must |
| FR-25 | Stop and target orders shall be OCO-linked when supported by the broker. If broker-native OCO is unavailable, the system shall cancel the sibling order immediately after either order fills. | Must |
| FR-26 | If protective-order placement fails or cannot be confirmed, the system shall attempt an immediate market exit and mark the session as an operational failure. It shall not continue unprotected by default. | Must |
| FR-27 | A partial fill shall create protection for the filled quantity and shall update protection when additional quantity fills. | Must |
| FR-28 | After a confirmed entry, the unfilled opposite-side entry shall be cancelled. If cancellation cannot be confirmed, the system shall continue monitoring broker orders and shall reject any additional local entry. | Must |
| FR-29 | The strategy shall reject all further entries after any broker-confirmed fill, regardless of whether the position later closes before 10:00 IST. | Must |
| FR-30 | The system shall record order IDs, request timestamps, acknowledgement timestamps, fill timestamps, fill quantities, average fill price, SL, TP, and cancellation results. | Must |

For stop-limit protection, the FRD implementation shall define a valid limit
buffer and the broker’s behavior when the market gaps through the trigger. A
stop-limit order must not be treated as equivalent to a guaranteed stop-market
exit.

### 5.5 Position Monitoring

| ID | Requirement | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| FR-31 | While the position is active, the system shall use WebSocket ticks for prompt SL and TP detection when the feed is available. | Must |
| FR-32 | The system shall use REST polling or the broker’s order/position state as a fallback when the WebSocket is unavailable. | Must |
| FR-33 | When both SL and TP conditions are observed in the same candle during backtesting, the engine shall use chronological ticks when available; otherwise it shall apply the configured conservative assumption and record it. | Must |
| FR-34 | A filled SL or TP shall close the position, cancel any remaining sibling order, and transition the session to `TRADE_CLOSED`. | Must |
| FR-35 | The system shall prevent duplicate exits caused by repeated ticks, repeated order updates, or simultaneous local and broker notifications. | Must |

### 5.6 Time-Based Exit State Machine

States are `UNINITIALIZED`, `SCANNED`, `WATCHING`, `ENTRY_PENDING`, `ACTIVE`,
`TRADE_CLOSED`, `ABORTED`, and `HARD_EXIT_PENDING`.

| ID | Transition or behavior | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| FR-36 | Before a successful 09:25 scan, the session shall remain `UNINITIALIZED` and shall not accept entries. | Must |
| FR-37 | After a valid scan, the session shall transition to `SCANNED`, then `WATCHING` at 09:30. | Must |
| FR-38 | At 09:45:00 IST, if the active position’s executable exit price is less than or equal to its average fill price, the system shall submit a market SELL and transition to `TRADE_CLOSED`. | Must |
| FR-39 | At 09:45:00 IST, if the executable exit price is greater than the average fill price, the system shall remain active until TP, SL, or hard exit. | Must |
| FR-40 | At 09:45:00 IST, all unfilled entry orders shall be cancelled, even if no position exists. | Must |
| FR-41 | At or after 10:00:00 IST, the system shall cancel all outstanding strategy orders and submit a market SELL for every remaining position quantity. | Must |
| FR-42 | The hard exit shall be attempted unconditionally, including when the position is profitable, flat, or losing. | Must |
| FR-43 | Exit failures shall be retried according to the operational retry policy and escalated as an unresolved position; the process shall not silently mark the trade closed. | Must |
| FR-44 | After exit confirmation, the system shall persist final fill details, realized P&L, exit reason, and all order-cleanup results. | Must |

At 09:45, “profit” means the current executable bid or broker-confirmed
market exit estimate is strictly greater than the average fill price. Fees and
taxes are excluded from this classification unless a broker net-P&L value is
available and explicitly configured.

## 6. Non-Functional Requirements

| ID | Requirement | Acceptance threshold |
|---|---|---|
| NFR-01 | Trigger-to-order submission latency | 95th percentile <= 500 ms under normal connectivity |
| NFR-02 | Fill-to-protection request latency | <= 1 second after fill notification |
| NFR-03 | Quote freshness | Ignore quotes older than 2 seconds |
| NFR-04 | WebSocket recovery | Reconnect within 5 seconds and retry at least 3 times with backoff |
| NFR-05 | Feed failure behavior | New entries are blocked when no valid quote path exists; active positions use broker-side protection and fallback polling |
| NFR-06 | Idempotency | Reprocessing an event shall not create a duplicate entry or exit |
| NFR-07 | Auditability | Every scan, tick decision, order event, state transition, and exception shall be timestamped and persisted |
| NFR-08 | Determinism | Backtests with identical inputs and configuration shall produce identical decisions and trade records |
| NFR-09 | Observability | Emit health, connection, stale-data, order, fill, and unresolved-position alerts |
| NFR-10 | Security | Credentials shall be supplied through environment or secret management and shall never be written to logs |

## 7. Edge Cases and Exception Handling

| Case | Required behavior |
|---|---|
| Spot quote unavailable at 09:25 | Retry within the scan retry policy; abort if no valid quote is obtained before the scan deadline |
| Option chain unavailable | Retry; abort without orders if the chain cannot be validated |
| No candidate within selection range | Abort and persist the reason |
| Stale or zero premium | Exclude the candidate; do not use it for selection or confirmation |
| CE and PE confirm on the same timestamp | CE wins; cancel or disable PE |
| Opposite entry cancellation fails | Maintain the global trade lock, monitor broker state, and alert; never submit a second entry |
| Market entry rejected | Release only the broker-confirmed pending state; do not retry after 09:45; record rejection |
| Partial market fill | Protect filled quantity immediately and reconcile remaining quantity |
| Stop placement fails | Attempt emergency market exit; mark operational failure |
| Target placement fails | Keep the protective stop only if confirmed; otherwise emergency-exit according to FR-26 |
| WebSocket disconnects | Use broker-side orders and fallback polling; block new entries if quote freshness cannot be maintained |
| Process restarts with open position | Reconcile position and orders before any new action; recreate missing protection or emergency-exit |
| Price gaps through stop-limit | Follow broker fill status; do not assume the stop filled; escalate unresolved quantity |
| Position is exactly flat at 09:45 | Exit immediately under FR-38 |
| Exit order fails at 10:00 | Retry and alert until broker confirms zero remaining quantity |
| Duplicate or out-of-order tick | Ignore for confirmation and log the data-quality event |

## 8. Execution Scenario Test Cases

| ID | Scenario | Expected result |
|---|---|---|
| TC-01 | Valid spot and option chain at 09:25 | One CE and one PE are selected and persisted |
| TC-02 | CE premium crosses 180 and remains above for 10 seconds | CE market BUY is submitted once |
| TC-03 | CE crosses 180 but returns to 180 or below | Confirmation resets; no entry until a new sustained crossing |
| TC-04 | PE confirms before CE | PE wins; CE entry is disabled or cancelled |
| TC-05 | CE and PE complete confirmation at the same time | CE is selected by tie-break rule |

| TC-07 | Entry partially fills | Protection is placed for the filled quantity and updated on additional fills |
| TC-08 | SL fills before target | Target is cancelled; position becomes `TRADE_CLOSED` |
| TC-09 | Target fills before SL | SL is cancelled; position becomes `TRADE_CLOSED` |
| TC-10 | Position is losing at 09:45 | Pending orders are cancelled and position is exited at market |
| TC-11 | Position is exactly at entry price at 09:45 | Position is exited at market |

| TC-16 | No valid option within 10 premium points of 180 | Session aborts without an entry |
| TC-17 | Both CE and PE entry orders are acknowledged, but only CE fills | PE is cancelled and no additional entry is permitted |
| TC-18 | Repeated fill or tick event is received | No duplicate position or exit order is created |

## 9. Required Audit Output

The system shall produce a session record containing:

- Trading date, strategy version, configuration, and timezone.
- Spot quote, ATM strike, expiry, candidate list, and selected CE/PE.
- Tick confirmation events and rejected signal reasons.
- Entry and exit order IDs, statuses, timestamps, quantities, and prices.
- Initial SL, target, actual fill prices, slippage, and realized P&L.
- State transitions, WebSocket status, fallback activation, and exceptions.
- A final session status: `TRADE_CLOSED`, `NO_TRADE`, `ABORTED`, or
  `UNRESOLVED_POSITION`.

Live and backtest outputs shall use the same field names. Backtests shall
produce both a trade-level table and event-level diagnostics so that every
entry and exit can be traced to its source quote or simulated tick.

## 10. Implementation Notes and Open Decisions

The existing repository has reusable abstractions for `Position`, pending
entries, market orders, protective stops, time windows, and tick processing.
The implementation must add or adapt the following strategy-specific pieces:

1. Broker option-chain discovery and quote freshness validation.
2. Dynamic CE/PE symbol selection at 09:25.
3. A global one-trade-per-day lock and OCO cancellation workflow.
4. Fixed target-order support and sibling-order cancellation.
5. Pre-entry watchlist WebSocket subscriptions.
6. The 09:45 profit classification and 10:00 hard-exit state machine.

Before live release, the team must confirm the broker’s supported order types,
native OCO behavior, partial-fill events, option-chain endpoint, tick size,
and stop-limit gap behavior. These broker-specific facts must be recorded in
the deployment configuration and covered by integration tests.

## 11. Test Coverage Quality Gate

The 9:30 OPT-Buy implementation shall not be approved for live trading until
the following coverage and behavior gates pass:

1. New strategy modules, including option selection, confirmation timing, the
   global trade lock, order protection, and time-based exits, shall achieve
   100% statement and branch coverage.
2. Every requirement from FR-01 through FR-44 shall map to at least one
   automated unit or integration test.
3. Every scenario from TC-01 through TC-18 shall be automated, except where a
   broker integration is unavailable; such scenarios must then have a broker
   contract test or an explicitly documented test double.
4. Coverage shall be measured in CI using the repository’s configured coverage
   command and shall fail the build if the required threshold is not met.
5. Existing shared modules shall retain their current coverage baseline or
   improve it. Excluding production code solely to reach 100% is prohibited.

The current repository baseline is not 100%: the existing test run passes 88
tests but reports 45.76% total coverage because the 9:30 OPT-Buy implementation
and its tests do not yet exist, and several existing modules are included in
the coverage scope. This is a release gate, not a claim that the current code
has already satisfied it.
